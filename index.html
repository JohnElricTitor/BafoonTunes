How BafoonsTunes works

  1. Pick sounds. Drag an instrument from the left onto the keyboard, or drag a drum onto a pad. Drag a whole kit onto the pads to fill all 8 at once.
  2. Play. Use your MPK mini, click the pads and keys, or use your computer keyboard.
  3. Record. Your song is made of layers. Add one with + MPK layer (notes from the MPK, pads and keys) or + Mic layer (a microphone) under the last layer, click a layer to select it, then press ●. Recording replaces what's in the selected layer, and each layer keeps looping, so you can build drums, then bass, then melody, then vocals. Click or drag along the top of the timeline to choose where playing and recording start.
  4. Pads. Right-click a pad to choose how it plays: Play while held, Play full sound (orange), Loop while held (purple) or Loop full sound (blue: press to start, press again to stop). To record your voice onto a pad, click the small ● on it, then hold the pad while you sing or play and let go to finish.
  5. Mix. Each track has mute (M), solo (S) and a volume slider. Drag an Audio effect (reverb, echo…) from the bottom of the left panel onto a layer, and click it on the layer to change how strong it is or remove it. The knobs K1–K8 shape the overall sound.
  6. Keep it. Save writes a .tunes project file you can open later. Export makes an MP3 or WAV of your loop.

Knobs

K1 Vibrato (also the joystick up) · K2 Keys volume · K3 Pads volume · K4 Master volume · K5 Tone · K6 Reverb · K7 Echo · K8 Release (how long keys ring). Double-click a knob to reset it. Right-click a knob, then turn a knob on your MPK to link them.

Your MPK mini

If the keys on screen don't line up with your MPK (for example after using its OCTAVE buttons), click Match keys above the on-screen keyboard and press the far-left key on your MPK. If pads show up as keys or land on the wrong pad, click Calibrate at the top: tap each pad once, then press the far-left key. Reset MPK settings forgets everything learned (pads, octave, knob links) and starts fresh.

Organising sounds

The Sounds panel has sections, which hold packs, which hold groups. Click any title to fold it. Right-click a title or a sound to add, rename, import into or delete. Drag sounds onto a group, pack or section title to move them, and drag pack or group titles onto the pads to load them. Deleting a built-in sound only hides it.
