Sample credits for Tunes
========================

Salamander Grand Piano
Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY 3.0. MP3 conversion from Tone.js audio.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

FluidR3 GM SoundFont
Electric piano, synth bass, saw lead, warm pad, strings, organ and nylon guitar from the Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont, © 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen, MIT license. Pre-rendered by Benjamin Gleitzman (midi-js-soundfonts, MIT, © 2012 Benjamin Gleitzman). Full license text in sounds/LICENSES.txt.
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

Sonic Pi samples
Drum and FX samples from Sonic Pi (sourced from Freesound contributors), CC0.
https://sonic-pi.net

Versilian Community Sample Library
Marimba, vibraphone, clap, rim, shaker and cymbal swell by Versilian Studios, CC0.
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

Growlybass
Electric bass by Karoryfer Samples, CC0.
https://karoryfer.com

Mirrors
VCSL and Growlybass files mirrored by danigb/samples.
https://github.com/danigb/samples
